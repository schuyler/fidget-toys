Fidget Toys

Experiments in one-shot gaming.

Dot Army

Two swarms of dots fight over spawn points. You vs the CPU, or another person online.

Wooden Ships

A two-ship sailing duel. You steer, the wind sets your speed. You vs the CPU.

Coreward Bound

Jump fleets blind across a spiral galaxy and race the CPU to bring three relics home from the core. Turn-based.

Star Troopers

Turn-based squad tactics. Plot your troopers' orders in secret, then watch the turn play out. You vs the CPU; still being balanced.
