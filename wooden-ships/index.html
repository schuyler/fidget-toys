How to play

  • You only steer. Drag sideways anywhere the way you want your bow to swing; the further you drag, the harder the rudder. Let go and it centres. The wind sets your speed. On a keyboard: ← → to steer.
  • You can't sail into the wind. The faint wedge ahead of your bow is the no-go zone: point into it and you slow to a stop. To get upwind, zigzag, and turn through the wind with speed on, or you'll stop head to wind (in irons) and drift backwards. Going backwards, the rudder works the other way.
  • Turning is a brake. The keel kills sideways motion, so a hard turn costs speed.
  • Two fire buttons, left and right as you see the screen, whichever way your ship is pointing. Each arrow shows where that side's guns point; the ring refills as they reload (5 s). Z / X on a keyboard.
  • Guns aim themselves, at the enemy as she is now, with no lead, and can swing 20° either way from square. Balls are slow: fire when she'll sail into them.
  • Cross her bow or stern and your whole broadside can land while none of her guns bear on you. That's the best move in the game.
  • Crew is hit points. Each hit kills one. A ship with none left strikes her colours, and the other side wins. Islands and the sea's edge stop you dead; shallows (the pale ring round islands) slow you down.
  • The sea is bigger than the screen. The view follows you; when the CPU is out of sight, an arrow on the edge points at it and says how far.
  • The wind shifts every so often without warning; the compass shows where it's blowing and how hard. With wind shadow on, a ship just downwind of another gets half the wind.
