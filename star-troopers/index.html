How to play

  • Take the three victory hexes on the centre line. A hex is yours when one of your troopers stands on it with no enemy next to it, and it stays yours until they take it back. Hold all three to win at once; after turn 12, whoever holds more wins. Wiping out the other squad also wins.
  • Each trooper has a pool of points, and every hit comes out of it as shock. A trooper whose shock reaches its pool is down. A trooper that isn't hit in a stage shakes off some shock (its Recovery).
  • A turn is two stages. In each, both sides plot orders in secret, then everything happens at once. A trooper spends up to 6 points a stage; what it doesn't spend in the first stage it can spend in the second, and what's left after that is wasted.
  • Plotting: drag the map to look around, pinch to zoom. Tap one of your troopers to select it: the cells it can reach are shaded, and enemies it can't see are dimmed. Drag it (or tap a shaded cell) to choose where it ends up. Tap an enemy to shoot at it. Tap your trooper again for dodge, overwatch, bombs and quick orders. Commit when you're done.
  • Shots come first, from where everyone starts the stage, so you shoot from where you stand, not where you're going. A shot's attack is points × Aim; the defence is the target's cover, range, the points it's plotted to spend moving, and its dodge points × Aim. The preview shows the expected loss if the target stands still and doesn't dodge, and how much dodge would cancel it. Then two dice decide.
  • Overwatch points shoot, once a stage, the nearest enemy that moves while in sight and within 6 cells. Enemies standing still don't set it off. A trooper it hits stops where it is.
  • Bombs need no line of sight. Tap Bomb… in your trooper's popover, then the cell to aim at; a bomb takes 3 or more points. It goes off after everyone moves, so a target that moves away escapes, and dodging near the target can make it miss. Blasts hit friends too.
  • Leaders: troopers in touch with a leader (★) shoot first, and their hits land before everyone else fires. The Vell are always in command.
  • The factions. Compact (pool 9, Aim 2, Recovery 3) bounce back if they get out of the fire. Orrin (12, 2, 1) do more and take more, but hits stick. Vell (6, 4, 2) shoot and dodge twice as well, with half the points to do it with.

Full rules, for the curious. An homage to Tom Walczyk's StarSoldier (SPI, 1977).
