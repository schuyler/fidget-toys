How to play

  • Drag your marker. Your dots stream toward it. That's your only control.
  • Dots face the way they last moved. A dot destroys an enemy in front of it, unless that enemy is facing it back. Then they freeze, glaring, until someone hits one from the side or behind.
  • Dots that reach your marker bunch up into a hedgehog, facing outward.
  • Park your marker on or beside a spawn point and your dots throw themselves at it, draining its ring. Empty the ring and it's yours: it starts making dots for you.
  • Wipe out the other side to win: take all their spawn points and destroy every last dot. Neutral spawns don't need capturing, and while you still have dots you're not out yet.
  • Tap anywhere to jump your marker there. Against the CPU you're blue.
