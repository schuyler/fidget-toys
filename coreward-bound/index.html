How to play

The goal

Deliver three relics to your settlements, or take every one of the CPU's. The relics lie at the centre of the galaxy. You're blue and you move first.

On your turn you can move as many stacks of fleets as you like, build, grow and research, then tap End turn. Next steps through the stacks and settlements that still have something to do; it greys out when you've seen them all.

Hex values are hidden until you land there. You see the CPU's stacks only roughly: 1, 2–3, 4–6 or 7+ fleets.

Jumping

Tap one of your stacks, then a highlighted hex. You see the odds of landing on target and every hex you might scatter to. Tap the hex again to cancel; press Jump to go. A one-hex hop always lands.

Missing scatters you. The further the jump, the worse the miss. Fleets that scatter far are lost on the way, and a stack is lost entirely if it lands off the map, or if a jump in or out of the core misses (red ✕ in the preview).

Safe range is 2 hexes, more with Reach techs. Longer jumps are allowed only from one of your working settlements, and miss more. Landing on target at one of them lets a stack jump again.

Settling and techs

Settle with 2 fleets, including ones that just arrived. Arms are worth 3–5, the periphery 1–3, the void between arms nothing: voids can't be settled.

A settlement makes RP equal to its value each turn. Spend RP to build fleets, a fleet to grow it, or fleets to research. One founded or taken this turn can't build, launch long jumps, chain jumps or take relics until your next turn.

Techs each add +1 to Reach (range and sight), Focus (jump rolls), Industry (fleets built) or Force (combat). Upkeep: 3 fleets per hex are free; each one beyond costs 1 RP a turn.

Relics, the core and fights

Relics appear on the centre hex and the six around it. Land on one and hold it through the CPU's turn to pick it up, then jump to any of your settlements. A stack carries one relic and can't chain-jump. A new relic appears 3 rounds after one is picked up.

The core is the bright middle of the galaxy. Nothing can be settled there, and every fleet that ends a turn there is destroyed on a roll of 1 in 6.

Fights happen when you jump onto a rival stack, and go on until one side is gone. Land on an undefended rival settlement to try to take it; stay to keep trying.

Dust lanes (brown walls) block sight and jumps, except one-hex hops and jumps between your own settlements. Beacons make jumps aimed at them more accurate.
